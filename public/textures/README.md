# Floor texture

`floor.webp` is a compressed derivative of the project owner's supplied `floor.jpg`.
The original remains unchanged in the project root. No generated imagery is used.

Preparation with Pillow: convert to RGB, crop `(624, 354, 2840, 1834)` at grout
lines to retain three columns and two rows of full tiles, resize to 1536 × 1024
with Lanczos, export WebP at quality 80, method 6 (EXIF metadata omitted).

In the scene the image covers 1.8 × 1.2 metres (approximately 60 cm per tile).
The texture repeats across the existing floor slab, preserving its footprint.
